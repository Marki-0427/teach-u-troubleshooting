Storage Almost Full

3 Move or back up large files.

Move personal videos, photos, and documents to a trusted external drive or cloud account after confirming the backup.

✓What to check:
  • Copy files first and verify they open from the backup.
  • Keep another backup for important files.
  • Avoid moving program or Windows folders manually.
