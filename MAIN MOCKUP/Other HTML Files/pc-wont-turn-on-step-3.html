PC Won't Turn On

3 Check the monitor.

Make sure the monitor is on and connected. Select the correct input, such as HDMI, DisplayPort, or VGA.

✓What to check:
  • Make sure the monitor is powered on.
  • Check the display cable and correct input.
  • A powered PC with a blank display may be a screen issue.
