Microphone Not Working

3 Check microphone permission.

Open Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone. Allow microphone access for the device and the app you are using.

✓What to check:
  • Enable microphone access.
  • Allow the relevant desktop or store app if listed.
  • Close and reopen the app.
