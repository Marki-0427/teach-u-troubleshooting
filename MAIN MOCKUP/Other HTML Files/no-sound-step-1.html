No Sound

1 Check your audio connections.

Plug in your headphones or speakers, check the audio-out port, turn the device on, and raise its volume.

✓What to check:
  • Check the headphone/speaker plug.
  • Confirm the speakers are powered on.
  • Try a different audio port if available.
