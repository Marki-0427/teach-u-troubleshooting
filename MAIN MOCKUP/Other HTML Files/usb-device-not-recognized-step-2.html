USB Device Not Recognized

2 Try another USB port.

Connect the device to another port directly on the PC, without a hub or extension if possible.

✓What to check:
  • Try a second port.
  • For desktop PCs, try a rear motherboard port.
  • Stop if a port is loose, damaged, or unusually hot.
