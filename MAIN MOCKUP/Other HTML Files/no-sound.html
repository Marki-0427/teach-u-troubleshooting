No Sound

Let's start with a few safe checks.

Before you start
  • Lower the volume: Reduce it before testing headphones or speakers.
  • Check the audio port: Use the correct jack and keep liquids away.
  • Check device power: Confirm speakers are on and their own volume is raised.
  • Do not open the PC case for a sound problem.
