Overheating

3 Clean the vents carefully.

Turn off and unplug your PC. Remove dust from external vents using an appropriate method and keep liquids away.

Do not open the power supply. Ask a technician if internal cleaning is needed.
✓What to check:
  • Shut down and unplug first.
  • Clean external vents or a removable filter gently.
  • Never open the power supply.
