Battery Not Charging

Start with simple power checks before changing battery settings.

Before you start
  • Use the correct charger for your laptop model.
  • Check the outlet and adapter for visible damage; do not use frayed or hot cables.
  • Keep liquids away from the laptop and charger.
  • Do not open the battery or power adapter.
