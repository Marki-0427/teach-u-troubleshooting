Battery Not Charging

1 Check the outlet and charger.

Connect the charger to a known-working wall outlet. Check that every plug is firmly connected and look for a charging light.

✓What to check:
  • Try another wall outlet.
  • Check both ends of the charging cable.
  • Stop using the charger if it smells burnt, sparks, or becomes unusually hot.
