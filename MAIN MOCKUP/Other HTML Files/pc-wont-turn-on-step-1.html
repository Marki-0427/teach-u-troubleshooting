PC Won't Turn On

1 Check the power source.

Check that the outlet works and the power cable is firmly connected to the wall and the back of the PC.

✓What to check:
  • Check that the outlet works.
  • Firmly reconnect both ends of the power cable.
  • Check any power strip switch.
