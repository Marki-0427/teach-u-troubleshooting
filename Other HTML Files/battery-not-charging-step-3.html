Battery Not Charging

3 Check battery and power settings.

When the laptop is on, select the battery icon and open Settings to review the battery status. Some laptops have a charge limit setting.

✓What to check:
  • Check whether a battery-protection mode is enabled.
  • Restart the laptop once.
  • Do not disable safety features just to force charging.
