Before you begin

Safety Guide

Follow these tips before troubleshooting your PC.

Power Off

Shut down and unplug the PC before opening it.

Stay Dry

Keep liquids away from your computer and workspace.

Be Gentle

Do not force cables, ports, or components.

Ask for Help

If a step feels unsafe, stop and contact a professional.
