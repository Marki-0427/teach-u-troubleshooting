Webcam Not Working

1 Check the camera privacy shutter.

Look for a sliding cover, camera switch, or keyboard privacy key. Make sure the lens is not covered.

✓What to check:
  • Open the physical shutter if present.
  • Check the camera indicator light.
  • Reconnect an external USB camera securely.
