PC Won't Turn On

Let's start with a few safe checks.

Before you start
  • Check the outlet safely: Test it with a known-working device if available.
  • Unplug before checking cables: Reconnect external power cables carefully.
  • Never open the power supply or insert anything into its vents.
  • Stop if you smell burning or see sparks; seek professional help.
