Printer Not Printing

Check the printer itself first, then verify how it connects to your computer.

Before you start
  • Make sure the printer has paper and ink or toner.
  • Do not reach inside a hot printer or touch moving parts.
  • Use the printer maker’s instructions for jams and consumables.
