Overheating

1 Check the fans and airflow.

Give your PC room for air to enter and exit. Clear vents and move the PC away from walls or objects blocking airflow.

✓What to check:
  • Keep the intake and exhaust vents unobstructed.
  • Notice whether fans spin or make unusual noises.
  • Move the PC away from walls or fabric.
