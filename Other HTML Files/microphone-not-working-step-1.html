Microphone Not Working

1 Check mute and connections.

Make sure the microphone is not muted by a physical switch, headset button, or keyboard key. Reconnect the headset if needed.

✓What to check:
  • Check the mute indicator.
  • Reconnect the headset securely.
  • Try a different compatible port if available.
