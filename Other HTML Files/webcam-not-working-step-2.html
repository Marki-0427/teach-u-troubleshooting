Webcam Not Working

2 Allow camera access.

Open Settings → Privacy & security → Camera. Turn on camera access and allow access for the app you want to use.

✓What to check:
  • Turn on camera access for the device.
  • Allow the relevant app.
  • Close and reopen the camera app.
