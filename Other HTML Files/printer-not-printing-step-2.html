Printer Not Printing

2 Check the connection.

For USB printers, check the cable. For Wi-Fi printers, confirm the printer and computer are on the intended network.

✓What to check:
  • Reconnect the cable without forcing it.
  • Check the printer’s Wi-Fi status.
  • Restart the printer only after checking its current job status.
