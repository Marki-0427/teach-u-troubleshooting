No Internet

Let's start with a few safe checks.

Before you start
  • Check other devices: See whether phones or other computers can connect before changing settings.
  • Do not reset the router: A factory reset may erase network settings.
  • Do not force Ethernet plugs: Press the connector latch before removing the cable.
  • Keep the router ventilated and dry.
