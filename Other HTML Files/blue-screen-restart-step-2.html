Blue Screen or Unexpected Restart

2 Disconnect new accessories.

Shut down Windows normally if possible, then disconnect recently added nonessential USB devices. Start the PC again.

✓What to check:
  • Remove recently added accessories one at a time.
  • Keep essential keyboard, mouse, and display connected.
  • Reconnect devices later to identify whether one triggers the issue.
