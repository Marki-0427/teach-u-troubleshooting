Overheating

4 Adjust settings or get more help.

Close demanding apps and check appropriate fan settings. Seek help if the PC still overheats, makes unusual fan noises, or shuts down.

✓What to check:
  • Close demanding applications.
  • Check whether airflow improves.
  • Seek service if it keeps overheating or shuts down.
