PC Is Slow

1 Check running applications.

Open Task Manager with Ctrl + Shift + Esc. Check CPU and Memory use, then close apps you recognize and don't need.

✓What to check:
  • Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc to open Task Manager.
  • Look at CPU, memory, and disk usage.
  • Close only apps you recognize and do not need.
