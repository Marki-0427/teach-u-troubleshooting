Screen Problems

1 Check monitor connection.

Check monitor power and reconnect both ends of the HDMI, DisplayPort, or VGA cable.

✓What to check:
  • Check monitor power and brightness.
  • Reconnect the display cable with devices off.
  • Try another port/cable if one is available.
